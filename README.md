# The Great Indian Reversal: 1960-2024

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes the dramatic economic rise and fall of Indian states using 60+ years of official government data.

## ❓ Question Explored
Which Indian states have experienced the most dramatic economic rise and fall since 1960?

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source:** Ministry of Finance, Government of India
- **Metric:** Relative Per Capita Income (National Average = 100)
- **Time Period:** 1960-61 to 2023-24
- **Coverage:** All Indian states

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 📉 The Biggest Fall: West Bengal
| Year | Relative Income | Status |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| 1960-61 | 127.5 | 27.5% ABOVE national average |
| 2023-24 | 83.7 | 16.3% BELOW national average |
| **DROP:** | **43.8 points** | **Most dramatic fall** |

### 📈 The Biggest Rise: Karnataka
| Year | Relative Income | Status |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| 1960-61 | 96.7 | 3.3% BELOW national average |
| 2023-24 | 180.7 | 80.7% ABOVE national average |
| **RISE:** | **84 points** | **Most dramatic rise** |

## 💡 The "Aha!" Moment
> In 1960, the average Bengali was **27% richer** than the average Indian.
> In 2024, the average Kannadiga earns **80% above average** - more than DOUBLE what the average Bengali earns.
>
> **This is one of the most dramatic economic reversals in modern Indian history.**
